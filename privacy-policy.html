Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

This policy explains how personal data submitted through the forms on www.kallang-close-residences.com is collected, used, stored and protected. It is written to align with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

What we collect

We collect only what you enter into the registration or enquiry forms on this site:

  • Your name, email address and mobile number
  • Your preferred contact day and time
  • The materials you have asked for — brochure, price list or a preview appointment
  • The unit types you indicate interest in

We do not ask for, and you should not submit, identification numbers, financial account details or any other sensitive personal data through this site.

How we use it

Your details are used solely to respond to your enquiry and to send you the project updates you registered for — the site plan, unit mix, price list, preview date and related information for Kallang Close Residences. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data.

Consent and withdrawal

Submitting a form on this site constitutes your consent to be contacted for the purposes above, including by phone, SMS, WhatsApp and email. You may withdraw that consent at any time by replying to any message you receive from us, or by writing to us using the contact details supplied in our correspondence. We will act on a withdrawal request within a reasonable period, after which we will stop sending you marketing communications.

Storage and retention

Form submissions are transmitted to a Google Sheet via Google Apps Script and are stored within Google Workspace. Access is restricted to the personnel handling enquiries for this project. We retain your data only for as long as it is needed for the purpose you provided it, or as required by law, after which it is deleted.

Third-party services and cookies

This site loads Google Fonts. Enquiries are sent through Google Apps Script to a Google Sheet. This website does not load an advertising pixel.

Accuracy and access

You may request access to the personal data we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by contacting us through any message you have received from us. We will respond within a reasonable period.

Security

We apply reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss or disclosure. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above will always reflect the current version.

About the information on this site

This is an independent information and enquiry website for Kallang Close Residences. Images on the website are artistic impressions, not actual project views. Confirm project details against official sales documents.
