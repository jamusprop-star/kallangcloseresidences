What is
Kallang Close Residences?

Kallang Close Residences is the working name for a private condominium planned on the Kallang Close land parcel, off Boon Keng Road in the Kallang area. Frasers Property Phoenix and MJR Investment won the 99-year lease at public tender on 10 April 2026. They paid $610,750,000. MJR Investment is owned by Mitsubishi Estate of Japan. No official project name has been announced yet, so this website uses the road name.

The neighbourhood around it is already built. Kallang MRT, Aperia Mall, the Upper Boon Keng food centre and the park along the river are all open today.

What the four bids tell you

The top two bids were $4.33 million apart on a $610 million site. Two experienced developers, working separately, arrived at nearly the same number. That suggests the winner paid close to what the market thought the site was worth.

The bottom two bids were much lower, the lowest by 12.21%. Two developers valued the land near $1,410 a square foot, and the other two nearer $1,270.

Land is the largest single cost in the project, so what the developers paid sets the floor for what the homes need to sell at. That is why the land rate is worth watching.

DISCOVER KALLANG CLOSE

A riverfront plot in
a neighbourhood already built.

There is no facility list yet. The site has a river frontage, three train stations within walking distance, a decades-old hawker centre and a stadium district that opened more than ten years ago.

Who is building it?

Frasers Property, which is listed in Singapore and built Riviere and Seaside Residences, together with Mitsubishi Estate of Japan. The two have never built a project together before, so there is no shared track record to look at.

Three stations, two lines, all running today.

Kallang on the East-West Line is about 440 m away in a straight line. Bendemeer on the Downtown Line is about 530 m. Lavender adds a third station within 800 m. All three are open today.

What the land contract requires.

URA requires at least 500 sq m of floor area on the site to be a childcare centre. Shops are capped at 115 sq m of floor area. The developers have also said they plan a riverside promenade open to the public. That is their stated plan, not a condition of the sale.

Compare before you commit.

A preview is a year away at the earliest. Use that time. Walk the area on a weekday. Look at what completed homes nearby actually sell for. Work out your budget before the preview.

THE INVESTMENT QUESTION

Is Kallang Close Residences
a good investment?

For own-stay, the location already delivers: three MRT stations within about 800 m on two working lines, an established neighbourhood, and almost no new private housing here for twelve years. For investment, a year’s rent would return about 2.4% of the purchase price before costs, using PropertyInsider’s $2,950 psf estimate and Kallang Riverside’s $5.90 psf monthly rent. The Singapore average is about 3.1%. The figure sets an estimated future price against rents being paid today, so revisit it against the actual price list.

OUR ASSESSMENT

This fits an owner-occupier better than an investor. The land cost was high, so the launch price is unlikely to open low. If you buy, plan to hold for seven to ten years, and make sure the monthly cost sits comfortably within your budget.

The case for it

The National Stadium and the indoor halls across the river opened in the mid-2010s, and an upgrade is running in stages to 2028. Many new launches ask you to believe in a masterplan. This one does not.

New private housing here is rare. This is the first state land parcel for private homes in the Boon Keng, Kallang Bahru and Kampong Bugis pocket in about twelve years, according to Frasers and Mitsubishi.

Most buyers will come from the neighbourhood. Agency researchers quoted by EdgeProp recorded 961 flat sales in Kallang and Whampoa between January 2025 and March 2026, and 807 in Geylang. Of those, 179 and 76 crossed a million dollars. Another 1,000 or so four-room flats in the estate reach the end of their minimum occupation period between 2026 and 2028.

The case against

The land was costly. At $1,415 psf ppr it was the second-highest rate in the Rest of Central Region over the previous two years, behind Tanjong Rhu Road at $1,455. A high land cost usually means a high starting price, so compare the launch price against what nearby projects sell for.

No primary school falls within 1 km. If Primary 1 priority matters to your decision, check the distances yourself before you commit.

Two nearby sites will compete for the same buyers. Tanjong Rhu Road, about 525 homes, went to CDL and Woh Hup in February 2026. Tanjong Rhu Close, about 505 homes, is on URA’s second-half 2026 sale list. Both are in the Kallang planning area, and all three projects could be selling at the same time.

Not every home will have a river view. Light industrial units occupy one side of the site, and some homes will face housing across the water instead. The site plan will show which.

What nearby homes sell for today

Both published estimates are higher than what nearby projects sell for today, including the freehold ones.

The two nearest new projects are freehold, which normally carries a premium. On these estimates the position is reversed: you would pay more, on a shorter lease, for a newer building.

For context, the median price for non-landed homes in the Kallang planning area was $1,958 psf in the first quarter of 2026, against $2,216 psf across the Rest of Central Region. According to ERA, the difference reflects limited new supply, with just three launches in Kallang since 2022.

Medians average across floors and unit sizes. When the real price list arrives, compare like with like: same size, same facing, same floor.

Four things to ask for before you decide

  1. The price of your unit, not the starting price. Advertised starting prices usually belong to the lowest floors and the least popular facings. Ask for the price of the exact unit you want.
  2. Recent sales at completed projects nearby. Check recent sales records for homes similar in size, floor and lease. Those give you a fair reference point for the launch price.
  3. All the costs between signing and moving in. You pay in stages as construction progresses. Maintenance, property tax and full mortgage payments begin once the home is completed.
  4. Your exit or rental plan. Work out how you would sell or rent it, then check the numbers still work if the sale takes longer or the rent comes in lower.

PropertyInsider published a range of $2,950 to $3,330 psf in August 2026. The gap comes mostly from different assumptions about the developer's profit margin.

How much would a 3-bedroom at Kallang Close Residences cost?

No prices exist yet. For a three-bedroom of about 950 sq ft, the published estimates suggest $2.56m to $3.16m. Both the price and the size are assumptions.

What is the tenure of Kallang Close Residences?

A 99-year lease, running from 10 April 2026, the date the site was awarded. So the lease started running in April 2026. The sale documents will state how many years remain.

Which MRT stations are near Kallang Close Residences?

Three stations are within about 800 m of the site, on two operating lines. Kallang on the East-West Line is about 440 m away. Bendemeer on the Downtown Line is about 530 m. Lavender, also on the East-West Line, is about 780 m. Boon Keng on the North East Line is a fourth option at about 1.03 km. These are straight-line distances, so the real walk is longer.

Is there a primary school within 1 km of Kallang Close Residences?

No. The nearest is Bendemeer Primary School, about 1.12 km away on research data. It is just outside the 1 km ring that gives priority in Primary 1 registration. Hong Wen School, Farrer Park Primary and Geylang Methodist (Primary) are all in the 1 km to 2 km band. Vacancies and ballots change every year, so check the current figures on MOE’s vacancies page. Check the official distance on MOE SchoolFinder once the development has an address.

What does the land contract require the developer to build?

URA set two conditions on the site. At least 500 sq m of floor area must be used for a childcare centre, and buildings cannot rise more than 110 m above sea level. ERA's research adds that shops are capped at 115 sq m. The developers have also mentioned plans for a public riverside promenade.

Are the images on this page actual views of the development?

No. Every image here is an artistic impression. They show the kind of setting a riverfront project can have, not the approved design, facilities, finishes or views at Kallang Close Residences. The floor plans shown are illustrative layouts, not the developer's plans.

Does registering interest reserve a unit at Kallang Close Residences?

No. The form sends us your details and what you are looking for. It does not hold a unit for you or give you priority in a ballot. Units are allocated through the developer's own booking procedure, which has not been published.
